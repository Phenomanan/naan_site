01 Profile

About Me

My path through security has been a tour of the whole stack. I started out building network defenses at NIKSUN (malware sandboxes and DDoS mitigation), spent nearly seven years at Etsy running detection engineering, SIEM infrastructure, and incident response (including a stint leading the SecOps team), and now I’m building AI-native security at Handshake.

The common thread: I like owning hard problems end to end, from the pipeline design to the dashboard to the conference talk explaining how it all works. I care about making security something engineers actually want to work with. When I’m not at a keyboard, I’m probably training for a race or hunting down the best dumplings in NYC (see Side Quests).
